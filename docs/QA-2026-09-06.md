## Dependency note

`npm audit --omit=dev` reports two high-severity denial-of-service advisories in the transitive `image-size` dependency of PptxGenJS. Upstream has no patched release at the time of this record. That Node-only parser is disabled from the browser bundle, is not invoked by the export path, and the app does not accept the affected ICNS/JXL/HEIF formats. No reachable production path was found.
